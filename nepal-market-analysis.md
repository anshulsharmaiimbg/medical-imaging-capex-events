# Nepal Medical Imaging Market Analysis & Sales Strategy

## Executive Summary
Nepal’s imaging market is concentrated in Kathmandu Valley and a small number of provincial hubs, with growth driven by private multi-specialty hospitals, teaching institutions, oncology demand, and diagnostic chain expansion. For United Imaging through Meta Healthcare, the near-term win strategy is **Tier-1 anchor accounts + modality-led replacement deals (CT/DR first), followed by MRI and oncology-led MI in select sites**.

This plan prioritizes high-probability sites, maps competitor penetration, and aligns United Imaging product families to Nepal’s budget, throughput, power, service, and installation constraints.

---

## 1) Market Overview & Insights

### Current Market State
- Installed base is dominated by multinational incumbents (GE, Siemens, Philips, Canon/Toshiba), especially in CT/MR at flagship hospitals.
- DR and ultrasound adoption is broader across private hospitals and diagnostics due to lower capex and faster ROI.
- PET-CT / advanced MI remains limited to top oncology and tertiary centers.

### Market Dynamics (Directional)
- **Demand growth drivers**: NCD burden (oncology, cardiology, neurology), trauma, medical tourism, private insurance penetration, and urban referral concentration.
- **Procurement behavior**: mix of outright purchase, staged payments, and occasional donor/public tenders.
- **Most actionable modality sequence**:
  1. DR and mid-slice CT (faster replacement cycles)
  2. 1.5T MRI in tertiary/private expansions
  3. PET-CT and high-end MRI/CT for flagship centers

### Healthcare Infrastructure Landscape
- **Primary concentration**: Kathmandu Valley (Kathmandu/Lalitpur/Bhaktapur).
- **Secondary hubs**: Pokhara, Bharatpur (Chitwan), Biratnagar, Birgunj, Nepalgunj, Dharan.
- **Facility archetypes**:
  - Tertiary teaching hospitals and corporate hospitals
  - Dedicated diagnostic imaging centers
  - Provincial/regional hospitals with constrained budgets and service capacity

### Regulatory Environment (Imports/Installation)
- Market access is distributor-led and compliance-sensitive; import, registration, and post-install support readiness are critical pre-sales gates.
- Practical operating requirements include:
  - Product registration/authorization workflows
  - Radiation safety and shielding compliance (site-specific)
  - Electrical quality/backup readiness, HVAC, and room civil readiness
  - Biomedical engineering training and uptime SLAs

- Uptime-guarantee + training-led differentiation where incumbent service quality is inconsistent.
- Faster installation and financing flexibility as commercial wedge.

## 4) United Imaging Product Positioning for Nepal

### Recommended Portfolio Fit by Segment
| Segment | Preferred UIH Positioning |
|---|---|
| Tier-1 tertiary/corporate | Flagship CT/MR + premium DR + selective MI |
| Tier-2 mid-sized hospitals | Mid-range CT + 1.5T MR + DR bundles |
| Tier-3 clinics/emerging facilities | Entry DR + ultrasound, future CT pathway |

### Modality-Level Guidance (Model-family approach)
- **CT**: prioritize mid/high throughput configurations for Tier-1 and value CT for Tier-2.
- **MR**: 1.5T as primary commercial workhorse; 3T targeted only at select flagship academic/corporate sites.
- **DR**: strongest land strategy (high install velocity, easy replacement economics).
- **MI**: target only oncology leaders and high-volume referral centers.
- **Ultrasound**: attach to DR/CT-led institutional entry.

### Pricing Strategy
- Use **3-track pricing**:
  1. Premium track (flagship sites, advanced applications)
  2. Volume-value track (Tier-2 bundle-led)
  3. Entry track (DR/US first, upgrade roadmap attached)
- Commercial levers: financing, warranty extensions, uptime SLA, application training credits.

## 7) Product Fit Analysis & Replacement Opportunities

### Installed-Base Replacement Logic
- **CT replacement triggers**: aging scanners, high maintenance burden, throughput bottlenecks.
- **MR replacement triggers**: image quality gaps, patient comfort constraints, exam-time inefficiencies.
- **DR replacement triggers**: detector downtime, workflow delays, poor dose/image balance.

### Performance vs Cost Positioning
- Position United Imaging as **high-performance at lower lifecycle cost**, not only lower capex.
- Emphasize measurable outcomes:
  - Throughput per day
  - Uptime % and service turnaround
  - Dose efficiency / image quality consistency
  - Cost per exam over 5 years

### Technical Advantage Narrative (sales-ready)
- AI-enabled workflow assistance and protocol optimization
- Broad portfolio for bundled procurement and standardization
- Scalable service model through distributor-local + OEM-regional support stack
